SUBJECT: a FAR-DISTANCE PARALLAX LAYER for a side-scrolling game: a wide horizontal row of distant silhouettes from the interior of a mirror tower at night, silver and violet palette: tall ornate mirror frames in tarnished silver, cracked mirrors reflecting nothing, thin gothic pillars, hanging crystal chandeliers, torn purple drapes, gargoyle statues, shards of glass — a broken mirror leaning, hanging chains, a spiral stair fragment, a curtain — drawn as a silhouette-like layer in 4 to 6 dark violet tones with cold silver rim light. The objects stand on an invisible ground line at the bottom edge of the canvas and occupy the LOWER 45% of the canvas. STRICT KEY: the background behind, between and BELOW the objects must be the flat pure green #00FF00 only — do NOT paint any floor, wall plane, sky, fog, frame, border or panel; the only non-green pixels are the objects themselves. Leave a clear GREEN GAP (no objects) at both the LEFT and RIGHT edges of the row so several rows can be placed side by side in any order. No characters, no text, no labels, no captions.
