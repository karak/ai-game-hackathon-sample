SUBJECT: BOSS "Serpent of Tears" — the HEAD only, facing LEFT, two frames side by side (same scale). A huge serpent head of pale teal porcelain-like scales with a big red hair ribbon tied behind its head and enormous wet, glassy, weeping eyes (cute) — but tears mixed with blood stream from the eyes down the scales, and the gums are exposed with cracked fangs (horrific). FRAME 1 = mouth closed, eyes wide and sad, tears streaming. FRAME 2 = mouth wide open in a strike, showing the fangs, a severed porcelain doll arm caught between the teeth, blood-tears spraying. The neck is cut off flat at the RIGHT edge of each head so a body segment can attach there.
