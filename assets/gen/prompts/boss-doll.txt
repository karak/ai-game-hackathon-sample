SUBJECT: BOSS "Weeping Doll Dorothy", standing, facing LEFT, one frame. A large antique porcelain doll: golden ringlet hair, cracked white porcelain face with huge glassy blue eyes, blue Victorian dress with white apron and lace, black shoes, small pale hands — but black tear tracks run from her eyes into pools of glowing green acid tears, one arm socket is torn open with red blood and stuffing, and the porcelain has spider-web cracks. Imposing but doll-cute.
