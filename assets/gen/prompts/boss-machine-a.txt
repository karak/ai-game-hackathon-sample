SUBJECT: BOSS "The Dollmaker's Machine", IDLE pose, facing LEFT, ONE figure filling about 90% of the canvas height. A huge antique brass sewing-machine automaton standing TALL on a rolling base: a lantern eye at the top, a lace-trimmed brass body, a giant needle arm raised high, spools of red thread as shoulders, a conveyor tray at its front, a cute mechanical face with rosy painted cheeks — but half-finished doll bodies are stitched into its frame, blood-soaked cotton spills from its seams, and the needle arm drips. Wheels on the baseline.
