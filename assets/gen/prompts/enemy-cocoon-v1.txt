PIXEL GRID CONTRACT (mandatory):
- Treat the 1024x1024 canvas as a grid of 128x128 cells. ONE CELL = 8x8 screen pixels = ONE logical pixel of the sprite.
- Every cell is filled with exactly one flat color. Never draw anything smaller than a cell: no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no sub-cell lines, no blur. Cell edges must align to multiples of 8 px.
- Think of it as a genuine 16-bit sprite magnified 8 times.

STYLE (strict): 16-bit SNES-era Japanese RPG pixel art in the manner of 1995 Square games (Secret of Mana, Chrono Trigger, Romancing SaGa 3). Chibi proportions 2.5-3 heads tall. Each material has 3-4 shade steps (shadow / base / light / highlight). Outlines are 1 cell wide, dark plum or dark brown (never pure black). Limited palette: 12-15 colors on the character.

BACKGROUND: completely flat solid pure green #00FF00 filling the whole canvas. No floor, no shadow, no glow, no frame, no text, no signature.
