PIXEL GRID CONTRACT (mandatory):
- Treat the 1024x1024 canvas as a grid of 128x128 cells. ONE CELL = 8x8 screen pixels = ONE logical pixel of the sprite.
- Every cell is filled with exactly one flat color. Never draw anything smaller than a cell: no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no sub-cell lines, no blur. Cell edges must align to multiples of 8 px.
- Think of it as a genuine 16-bit sprite magnified 8 times.

STYLE (strict): 16-bit SNES-era Japanese RPG pixel art in the manner of 1995 Square games (Secret of Mana, Chrono Trigger, Romancing SaGa 3). Chibi proportions 2.5-3 heads tall. Each material has 3-4 shade steps (shadow / base / light / highlight). Outlines are 1 cell wide, dark plum or dark brown (never pure black). Limited palette: 12-15 colors on the character.

BACKGROUND: completely flat solid pure green #00FF00 filling the whole canvas. No floor, no shadow, no glow, no frame, no text, no signature.


SIZE CONTRACT for this sprite:
- Logical box: 60 cells wide x 160 cells tall (= 480 x 1280 screen px at 8 px per cell).
- The drawing must fit inside that box and should use most of it (at least 136 cells of the 160-cell height).
- Exactly 2 figures side by side in one row, separated by clear gaps of green, the group centered horizontally. Same scale and same palette for every figure.
- Feet rest on the baseline y = 960 px (that is 8 cells above the bottom edge). Nothing below the baseline.

SUBJECT: a TOWERING PILLAR OF WAX AND FIRE for a platformer magic effect, two frames side by side, standing on the ground, EXTREMELY TALL AND NARROW: the column must fill the whole height of the box (about 150 cells tall) and be only about 30 cells wide — a proportion of 1 wide to 5 tall, like a cathedral candle taller than a person.
Lower two thirds: a thick column of cream-colored church wax with ropes of molten wax running down its sides and a pooled wax base. Upper third: one continuous roaring orange flame with a white-hot core, as tall as the wax below it is high.
FRAME 1 = the flame is a tall narrow spear leaning slightly right, two wax drips on the left side of the column.
FRAME 2 = the flame is broader with a forked tip leaning left, three wax drips on the right side, one drop falling free near the base (clear flicker between the frames).
Palette: cream, warm grey, amber, orange, white-hot core, dark brown outline. No floor, no shadow, no text. Each frame is one connected column that runs from the baseline to the top of the box.
