SUBJECT: BOSS "The Mirror Queen", ONE figure filling about 90% of the canvas height, facing LEFT. A tall elegant queen made of living mirror glass: a silver crown, a gown of overlapping mirror shards reflecting purple light, long silver hair, a graceful painted smile (cute) — but half of her face is a shattered pane with a hollow behind it, blood seeps between the shards of her gown, and she holds a broken mirror frame like a scepter with a doll's hand caught in the glass (horrific). Standing on the baseline.
