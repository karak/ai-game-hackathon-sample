PIXEL GRID CONTRACT (mandatory):
- Treat the 1024x1024 canvas as a grid of 128x128 cells. ONE CELL = 8x8 screen pixels = ONE logical pixel of the sprite.
- Every cell is filled with exactly one flat color. Never draw anything smaller than a cell: no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no sub-cell lines, no blur. Cell edges must align to multiples of 8 px.
- Think of it as a genuine 16-bit sprite magnified 8 times.

STYLE (strict): 16-bit SNES-era Japanese RPG pixel art in the manner of 1995 Square games (Secret of Mana, Chrono Trigger, Romancing SaGa 3). Chibi proportions 2.5-3 heads tall. Each material has 3-4 shade steps (shadow / base / light / highlight). Outlines are 1 cell wide, dark plum or dark brown (never pure black). Limited palette: 12-15 colors on the character.

BACKGROUND: completely flat solid pure green #00FF00 filling the whole canvas. No floor, no shadow, no glow, no frame, no text, no signature.


SIZE CONTRACT for this sprite:
- Logical box: 96 cells wide x 120 cells tall (= 768 x 960 screen px at 8 px per cell).
- The drawing must fit inside that box and should use most of it (at least 102 cells of the 120-cell height).
- Exactly 2 figures side by side in one row, separated by clear gaps of green, the group centered horizontally. Same scale and same palette for every figure.
- Feet rest on the baseline y = 960 px (that is 8 cells above the bottom edge). Nothing below the baseline.
- PART SIZES (count them on the grid; 1 cell = 1 logical pixel). Every frame must reproduce these part sizes within 2 cells, whatever the pose:
    * TOTAL HEIGHT from the top of the sprite (hat tip, or hair top when hatless) to the boot sole: 100 cells when standing upright
    * head: 40 cells from the top of the hair to the chin
    * hair: 40 cells wide at its widest
    * eyes: 8 cells tall
    * the eyes start 18 cells below the top of the hair
    * torso (collar to waist): 20 cells
    * shoulders including the puff sleeves: 26 cells wide
    * hips (waist to where the fish tail begins): 10 cells
    * fish tail from the hips to the tip of the tail fin: 36 cells measured along the tail
    * tail fin: 24 cells wide at its widest
- A pose may fold the body (crouch, tuck, lean) and so reduce the TOTAL height, but it must never enlarge the head, hair, hat or eyes. If the drawing is coming out with a bigger head or a wider brim than the numbers above, it is wrong.

SUBJECT: "Mermaid Doll" enemy, facing LEFT, two frames side by side. A porcelain mermaid doll with big glassy teal eyes, seashell top and a pink hair ribbon (cute) — but her fish tail is skinned raw, showing red flesh and white spine bones between torn scales, and her mouth is full of rusty nail teeth (horrific).
FRAME 1 = surfacing pose, drawn as a COMPLETE figure from the ribbon down to the tail fin: sitting upright, arms resting at her sides with the hands in front of the hips, mouth closed in a doll smile, the torso, hips and the whole skinned tail visible below the seashell top, tail curled down and forward so the tail fin rests on the baseline. Do NOT stop the drawing at the belly — the hips and the tail must be drawn.
FRAME 2 = biting lunge: body leaning forward, mouth wide open showing the nail teeth, one arm reaching forward, tail arched up behind with water drops flying (drops within 6 cells of the body). Same head size and same tail length as FRAME 1.
Both frames must be one connected shape each. No waterline, no water surface, no splash pool — only the character on the green.
