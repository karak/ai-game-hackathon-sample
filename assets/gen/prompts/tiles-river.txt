SUBJECT: a TERRAIN TILE STRIP for a side-scrolling platformer set on the muddy bank of a river of tears in the rain. A wide horizontal strip: the walkable ground surface along the top edge of the strip (wet dark mud with short blue-green grass, 1-cell details: pebbles, eyelash-shaped reeds, tiny broken doll fingers, puddles), then the underground fill below (dark wet earth with roots, grey stones and a few embedded doll parts). The strip must be SEAMLESSLY tileable horizontally and its bottom rows must also tile vertically as plain fill. Place it in the LOWER HALF of the canvas; the upper half is the flat green key color. No characters, no text.
