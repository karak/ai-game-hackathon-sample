Leave a clear GREEN GAP (no objects) at both the LEFT and RIGHT edges of the row so that several such rows can be placed side by side in any order. No characters, no text. Do NOT draw the moon, sky, stars or clouds in this layer — only the scenery objects; everything above them stays green.
