SUBJECT: BOSS "The Dollmaker's Machine", facing LEFT, two frames side by side (same scale). A huge antique brass sewing machine automaton on a rolling base: a lantern eye, a lace-trimmed brass body, a giant needle arm, spools of red thread as shoulders, a conveyor tray at its front (cute mechanical face with rosy painted cheeks) — but half-finished doll bodies are stitched into its frame, blood-soaked cotton spills from its seams, and the needle arm is dripping (horrific). FRAME 1 = idle: needle arm raised, lantern eye calm. FRAME 2 = attack: needle arm slammed down to the left with a spray of pins and red thread, eye glowing, a doll body flung forward. Feet/wheels on the baseline.
