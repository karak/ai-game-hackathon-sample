SUBJECT: a FAR-DISTANCE PARALLAX LAYER for a side-scrolling game: a wide horizontal row of distant objects from a snowy doll workshop interior at night, grey stone and warm amber lamplight: tall arched windows showing falling snow, wooden workbenches, hanging half-finished porcelain dolls on hooks, spools of red thread, giant scissors, brass gears and pulleys, cotton stuffing drifting like snow — tall shelves stacked with doll heads, a large wall clock, hanging chains with hooks, a giant spool — drawn as a silhouette-like layer in 4 to 6 dark grey-violet tones with warm amber rim light. The objects stand on an invisible ground line at the bottom edge of the canvas and occupy the LOWER 45% of the canvas. STRICT KEY: the background behind, between and BELOW the objects must be the flat pure green #00FF00 only — do NOT paint any floor, wall plane, sky, fog, frame, border or ground; the only non-green pixels are the objects themselves. Leave a clear GREEN GAP (no objects) at both the LEFT and RIGHT edges of the row so several rows can be placed side by side in any order. No characters, no text, no labels, no captions.
