PIXEL GRID CONTRACT (mandatory):
- Treat the 1024x1024 canvas as a grid of 128x128 cells. ONE CELL = 8x8 screen pixels = ONE logical pixel of the sprite.
- Every cell is filled with exactly one flat color. Never draw anything smaller than a cell: no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no sub-cell lines, no blur. Cell edges must align to multiples of 8 px.
- Think of it as a genuine 16-bit sprite magnified 8 times.

STYLE (strict): 16-bit SNES-era Japanese RPG pixel art in the manner of 1995 Square games (Secret of Mana, Chrono Trigger, Romancing SaGa 3). Chibi proportions 2.5-3 heads tall. Each material has 3-4 shade steps (shadow / base / light / highlight). Outlines are 1 cell wide, dark plum or dark brown (never pure black). Limited palette: 12-15 colors on the character.

BACKGROUND: completely flat solid pure green #00FF00 filling the whole canvas. No floor, no shadow, no glow, no frame, no text, no signature.


SIZE CONTRACT for this sprite:
- Logical box: 110 cells wide x 130 cells tall (= 880 x 1040 screen px at 8 px per cell).
- The drawing must fit inside that box and should use most of it (at least 110 cells of the 130-cell height).
- Exactly 2 figures side by side in one row, separated by clear gaps of green, the group centered horizontally. Same scale and same palette for every figure.
- Feet rest on the baseline y = 960 px (that is 8 cells above the bottom edge). Nothing below the baseline.
- PART SIZES (count them on the grid; 1 cell = 1 logical pixel). Every frame must reproduce these part sizes within 2 cells, whatever the pose:
    * TOTAL HEIGHT from the top of the sprite (hat tip, or hair top when hatless) to the boot sole: 103 cells when standing upright
    * head: 40 cells from the top of the hair to the chin
    * hair: 40 cells wide at its widest
    * eyes: 6 cells tall
    * the eyes start 20 cells below the top of the hair
    * torso (collar to waist): 20 cells
    * shoulders including the puff sleeves: 30 cells wide
    * skirt (waist to hem): 24 cells
    * skirt hem: 44 cells wide when hanging still
    * legs (hem to sole, socks + boots): 18 cells
    * boots: 10 cells tall
    * each boot: 12 cells long
- A pose may fold the body (crouch, tuck, lean) and so reduce the TOTAL height, but it must never enlarge the head, hair, hat or eyes. If the drawing is coming out with a bigger head or a wider brim than the numbers above, it is wrong.
