SUBJECT: a TERRAIN TILE STRIP for a side-scrolling platformer set in a bone-built amusement park at night. A wide horizontal strip: the walkable ground surface along the top edge of the strip (a pavement of skull tiles and rib planks with confetti, popcorn, blood drips and neon light spots as 1-cell details), then the underground fill below (packed bones and dark soil with buried teeth and a few neon cables). The strip must be SEAMLESSLY tileable horizontally and its bottom rows must also tile vertically as plain fill. Place it in the LOWER HALF of the canvas; the upper half is the flat green key color. No characters, no text.
