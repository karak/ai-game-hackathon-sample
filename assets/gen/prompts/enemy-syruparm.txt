SUBJECT: "Syrup Arm" enemy for a candy-forest stage, rising out of a pool of syrup, facing LEFT, two frames side by side. A slender arm made of thick amber-pink syrup reaching up out of a glossy puddle; the syrup drips off the fingers in long strings and the surface of the puddle bulges. Inside the translucent syrup you can see swallowed candies and one small white bone.
FRAME 1 = the arm is half risen from the puddle, elbow still submerged, fingers curled downward, three drip strings.
FRAME 2 = the arm is fully extended and swiping to the LEFT, fingers spread like a claw, the puddle stretched into a cone beneath it, five drip strings and two flying droplets close to the hand.
The puddle base must sit on the baseline and be wider than the arm. No floor line, no shadow, no text. Each frame is one connected shape.
