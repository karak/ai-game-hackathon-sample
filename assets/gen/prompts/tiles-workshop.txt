SUBJECT: a TERRAIN TILE STRIP for a side-scrolling platformer set inside a snowy doll workshop. A wide horizontal strip: the walkable floor surface along the top edge of the strip (dark wooden planks with a thin layer of drifted cotton snow, 1-cell details: nails, pins, red thread scraps, a doll finger), then the underground fill below (grey stone blocks and brick with a few brass pipes and gears embedded). The strip must be SEAMLESSLY tileable horizontally and its bottom rows must also tile vertically as plain fill. Place it in the LOWER HALF of the canvas; the upper half is the flat green key color. No characters, no text.
