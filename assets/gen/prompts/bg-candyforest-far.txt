SUBJECT: a FAR-DISTANCE PARALLAX LAYER for a side-scrolling game: a wide horizontal band of distant scenery — a candy forest rotting in a poison swamp at dusk: giant lollipop trees with faded stripes, huge spotted mushrooms, candy-cane trunks with mold, dripping syrup, dead gumdrop bushes — drawn as a silhouette-like layer in 4 to 6 dark tones with soft rim light. TALL BAND: the scenery is LARGE — the tallest objects reach 90% of the canvas height and the objects occupy the LOWER 90% of the canvas; the upper part must be the flat green key color. Leave a clear GREEN GAP (no objects) at both the LEFT and RIGHT edges of the row so that several such rows can be placed side by side in any order. No characters, no text. Do NOT draw the moon, sky, stars, clouds or a ground plane in this layer — only the scenery objects; everything above and between them stays flat pure green #00FF00. This is VARIANT A.
