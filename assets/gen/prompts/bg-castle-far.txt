SUBJECT: a FAR-DISTANCE PARALLAX LAYER for a side-scrolling game: a wide horizontal band of distant scenery — the interior of a sugar-white castle stained with blood: tall stone-and-frosting columns, gothic arched stained-glass windows dripping red, chains, candle chandeliers, torn pink banners — drawn as a silhouette-like layer in 4 to 6 dark tones with soft rim light. TALL BAND: the scenery is LARGE — the tallest objects reach 90% of the canvas height and the objects occupy the LOWER 90% of the canvas; the upper part must be the flat green key color. Leave a clear GREEN GAP (no objects) at both the LEFT and RIGHT edges of the row so that several such rows can be placed side by side in any order. No characters, no text. Do NOT draw the moon, sky, stars, clouds or a ground plane in this layer — only the scenery objects; everything above and between them stays flat pure green #00FF00. This is VARIANT A.
